# About

Macro Quant Lab is my personal research workspace for multi-asset and rates markets. Everything here is built on free, public data and runs on a schedule with GitHub Actions.

**What is here**

- **Daily Macro Desk**: about 45 market series, quality-checked every morning, summarised in a short brief with charts and a three-minute audio version.
- **Central Bank Decoder**: each policy statement redlined against the last one, with a tone score I evaluate against my own reading.
- **Model Portfolio Lab**: a paper-traded multi-asset portfolio with a written investment policy, monthly factsheets and Brinson attribution.
- **Notes**: short pieces in my own words, one view supported by three charts.

**How AI is used**: AI drafts, codes and challenges. It never decides, and an automated check rejects any AI-written sentence that states a number not in the day's data table.

**Disclaimer**: personal research on public data. Not investment advice, not an offer or solicitation, and no real money. Views are my own and not those of any employer. Data can be wrong or late; check the source before relying on it.
